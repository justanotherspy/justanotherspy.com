I spend a lot of time in Claude Code. More than I'd like to admit. garlic started as a one-liner in my shell config and grew into something I actually find useful: a small CLI for knowing where my session hours go.

It does three things, in this order: it watches, it summarises, and it gets out of the way. Anything beyond that is creep.

## why this exists

The honest answer is that I wanted a single sentence at the end of the day that told me what I'd actually done, separate from what I felt I'd done. Memory is a poor logger.

## what it isn't

It is not a productivity tool. It does not score your work. It does not categorise, gamify, or summarise into a Slack channel. The whole user interface is one line of output, on demand.

## what's next

Less than I'd like, more than I have time for. The repository is open; the rest is on [GitHub](https://github.com/).
